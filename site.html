Meet your physiotherapist

Mary.W, Bsc PT.

With over seven years of dedicated clinical experience across hospital and private practice settings, she specializes in restoring movement and improving quality of life for her patients. Her practice focuses on treating a wide range of conditions, including musculoskeletal injuries, chronic back and neck pain, neurological disorders, and post-operative orthopedic rehabilitation. Passionate about evidence-based care, she blends manual therapy, targeted exercise prescription, and patient education to design personalized recovery plans that help individuals return to their daily activities safely and pain-free.
